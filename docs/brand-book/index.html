01 · Brand Foundation

Who Alesvia is

Alesvia je vznikající český institut pro psychologickou stránku umělé inteligence — vzdělává veřejnost, školí organizace podle AI Actu a připravuje terapeuty na nová klinická témata.

Alesvia is an emerging Czech institute for the psychological side of artificial intelligence — educating the public, training organizations under the EU AI Act, and preparing therapists for new clinical topics.

Positioning

Alesvia must look like a modern public-interest institution — not a startup product and not a generic nonprofit template. The identity communicates institutional seriousness, clarity, composure, intellectual depth, and human-centered governance.

The four pillars

UnpluggedAktivní / Active

Healthy boundaries with conversational AI — clearly and without moralizing. AI is a tool, not a relationship.

Akademie / AcademyV přípravě / In preparation

AI literacy and humanly sustainable AI deployment for companies, schools, and public administration (incl. Article 4 of the EU AI Act). Revenue funds public-benefit programs.

Praktici / PractitionersV přípravě / In preparation

Training psychologists, psychiatrists, and therapists for the AI era.

Policy LabV přípravě / In preparation

Research and advocacy translating ethical frameworks into legislative and corporate guidelines.

Truthful language rule. Alesvia is being established as a registered institute (z.ú.); registration is in progress. Public materials must never claim completed registration, a trademark, or running services before they exist — use "vzniká", "v přípravě", "registrace probíhá".

02 · Logo Architecture

The architectural A

The mark is an architectural A that also reads as a threshold or portal. The outer form is the institution; the inner gold path is autonomy — a centerline of agency inside a larger system. It is deliberately not a shield, chat bubble, spark, brain, or circuit.

Primary symbol — light backgrounds
Reverse symbol — dark backgrounds
Horizontal lockup — the preferred formal treatment

Wordmark & lockup rules

  • The wordmark is Alesvia in a refined serif — no tagline inside the logo, no period.
  • Horizontal lockup for navbars, deck covers, one-pagers, partner documents.
  • Stacked lockup for ceremonial, centered use: PDF title pages, interstitials, profile headers.
  • Symbol alone for favicons, avatars, and compact placements; wordmark alone only in wide layouts where the symbol is established nearby.

04 · Typography

Institution and utility

Typography splits into two clear roles: serif for identity, major statements, and thesis-led emphasis; sans-serif for interface copy, navigation, metadata, and body structure. The current pairing is Playfair Display + Inter.

Typographic character

The brand should feel:

  • composed

05 · Usage Rules

How to use the mark

Preferred uses

  • Horizontal lockup for formal brand appearances.
  • Symbol alone for favicon, app icon, avatar, small UI.
  • On light backgrounds: ink outer form, gold centerline.
  • On dark backgrounds: reverse symbol or lockup; place the standard symbol on a paper tile when contrast is weak.
  • At small sizes use the symbol only; at medium and large sizes prefer the full lockup.

What to avoid

  • Do not stretch the symbol vertically or horizontally.
  • Do not recolor the symbol arbitrarily.
  • Do not replace the gold centerline with gradients or rainbow accents.
  • Do not drop the symbol into busy atmospheric gradients without contrast.
  • Do not use the mark as a decorative pattern until the identity matures.

Language & legal guardrails

  • Never present the mark or name as a registered trademark before clearance and registration are complete.
  • Never state that Alesvia is a registered institute — say "vzniká jako zapsaný ústav (z.ú.); registrace probíhá."
  • Retired sub-brand names (Mind, Proof, Compass, Watch, Advisory, Education) must not appear as standalone initiatives; their themes live inside the four pillars.
  • Before public launch at scale, follow the trademark clearance process in docs/brand-management.md.
